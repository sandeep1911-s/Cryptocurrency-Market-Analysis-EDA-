# 📈 Cryptocurrency Market Analysis (EDA)

## About the Project

This project is an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of six major cryptocurrencies: Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), BNB, Solana (SOL), XRP, and Tether (USDT). It uses daily historical market data from Yahoo Finance to study how these assets have behaved over time, looking at their prices, returns, trading volume, and risk.

> For educational purposes only. Not investment advice.

## How It Helps

- Shows how cryptocurrency prices have moved over time and which assets performed best or worst
- Measures risk through volatility and maximum drawdown, so returns are not judged on their own
- Reveals whether different cryptocurrencies tend to move together, which is useful for diversification
- Compares trading volume and price movement to understand market activity
- Turns raw market data into clear charts and summary tables that are easy to present
- Works as a reusable template: change the tickers or date range to analyze any other coins

### 💼 In Business Decision-Making

- **Risk management:** volatility and drawdown figures show how much value an asset has lost at its worst, helping companies set risk limits before holding or accepting crypto
- **Portfolio diversification:** the correlation analysis shows which assets move together, so businesses and investment teams can avoid concentrating risk in similar assets
- **Treasury and payments:** companies that hold or accept crypto can compare stablecoins and volatile coins to choose what suits their cash-flow needs
- **Performance comparison:** cumulative return and the hypothetical $1,000 investment chart make it easy to compare assets side by side in reports and presentations
- **Liquidity assessment:** trading volume shows how easily an asset can be bought or sold, which matters for larger transactions
- **Data-driven reporting:** exported CSV files and clear charts can be shared with managers, clients, or stakeholders to support decisions with evidence

### 👥 For Common People

- **Understand the risk:** shows in simple charts how sharply crypto prices can rise and fall, so people know what to expect before putting in money
- **Look beyond headline returns:** a coin that gained a lot may also have dropped sharply along the way, and the drawdown analysis makes this visible
- **Learn the difference between coins:** stablecoins like USDT behave very differently from coins like SOL or XRP, and the charts show this clearly
- **Avoid hype-driven decisions:** historical data gives a factual view instead of relying on social media or rumors
- **Learn data analysis:** beginners can use the notebook to learn how financial data is collected, cleaned, and analyzed with Python
